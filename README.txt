SHOUNEN DEN — SCROLL-ANIMATED STATIC WEBSITE

Open index.html in Chrome/Firefox to preview.

WHAT IS UPDATED
- Dark charcoal theme is locked consistently regardless of the computer’s light/dark setting.
- Neon lime, pink and blue accents.
- Scroll-linked motion for product panels, shirt illustrations, and the story panel in browsers supporting CSS scroll timelines.
- Intersection-observer reveal fallback for other browsers.
- Shirt silhouettes now have curved sleeve edges, smoother shoulder transitions, and a curved hem (not just rounded card containers).
- Rounded collection cards and labels.

EDITING
- Clothing concepts are inline SVG inside index.html. Search for product names: Void Runner Tee, Unwritten Tee, Blue Hour Tee.
- Product artwork silhouette is smoothed by the JavaScript function near the end of the file.
- Contact placeholders are hello@shounenden.example and “Add your Instagram handle”; replace them before publishing.
- The newsletter is a front-end demo and does not save submissions.

This is a static prototype; no server is required.
